Philosophy

Evaluation scores lie by default. Architecture is how you catch it.

On one of our own systems, automated evaluation scored 94% while real-world compliance was 22.2% — a gap that would have reached real users if no one had gone looking for it. That's the moment this company was built to prevent, for every team we work with.

"The most dangerous AI failure is not the one that crashes. It is the one that looks healthy while being wrong."
