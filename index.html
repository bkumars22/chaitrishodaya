Check yourself

How exposed is your AI? Find out in 60 seconds.

Eight yes-or-no questions, and a calculator for what wrong answers might be costing you. Nothing is sent anywhere; it all runs in your browser.

60-second AI risk check

Answer for one AI system you run or are building.

1. Before every release, do you test AI answers against a fixed set of questions with known correct answers?

2. Can every AI answer show the document and page it came from?

3. Is access checked before the AI searches your documents, not after?

4. Do you record which prompt and model version produced each answer?

5. Would you know within an hour if answer quality dropped in production?

6. Is personal data such as Aadhaar, PAN and account numbers masked before it reaches the AI or its logs?

7. Does a person approve rollbacks and other high-risk AI changes?

8. Have you tested your AI with misleading or hostile inputs?

    Questions

    Answers to what people ask first.

    What is AI Quality Engineering?

    AI Quality Engineering combines AI evaluation, behavioral testing, adversarial testing, monitoring, and continuous quality verification to improve the reliability of production AI systems — closing the gap between what an automated eval score reports and how a system behaves under real, adversarial use.

    Do you provide AI testing services?

    Yes. Chaitrishodaya provides AI testing for LLM applications, AI agents, RAG systems, and production AI platforms — including adversarial golden-dataset testing, automated test generation, and multi-framework test automation.

    Can you test AI agents?

    Yes. We evaluate AI agent behavior, tool usage, decision paths, reliability, security, and failure modes — including multi-agent systems built with LangGraph and CrewAI.

    Do you develop AI systems, not just test them?

    Yes. We design and build complete AI applications, AI agents, RAG platforms, and enterprise AI systems — eleven of which are designed, documented and demonstrable today.

    Can you help reduce AI infrastructure costs?

    Yes. We analyze model routing, prompt design, and pipeline efficiency to reduce AI operating cost while maintaining required quality — demonstrated at an 84% cost reduction on our own production system.
