Release pipeline

Every change earns its way into production

Prompts, models and retrieval settings are versioned like code. Nothing reaches all users until it beats the bar on real questions.

  1. Step 1Version the changePrompt, model or retrieval config gets an id and a reviewer, like code.
  2. Step 2Evaluation gate in CIScored on the golden dataset plus adversarial cases. Below 0.90, it stops.
  3. Step 3Canary on a slice5–10% of live traffic, compared with the current version on quality, cost and latency.
  4. Step 4Full rolloutPromoted only if the canary holds. Every answer carries the version that produced it.
  5. Step 5Auto-rollbackIf live scores drop, the last good version returns and a person signs off the fix.

What wins customer satisfaction

Every answer shows its sourcePeople trust what they can check. Citations with page numbers turn doubt into confidence.
It says "I don't know"One confident wrong answer costs more trust than ten honest refusals.
Quality never quietly dropsRelease gates and live monitoring keep yesterday's good answers good tomorrow.
Data stays safeAccess is checked before search and personal data is masked, so nothing leaks.
Fast, and fixed fastStreaming answers feel instant, and a bad release is undone in minutes.
A human is one click awayLow-confidence cases go to a person, and their fixes feed the next release.

How satisfaction is tracked: thumbs-up rate on answers, share of questions resolved without escalation, weekly active users, and a short survey each quarter.
