Privacy policy

What happens with your information when you visit this site or use the contact form.

This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens with your information when you visit chaitrishodaya.com or use the contact form on this site. We've kept it short on purpose — this site collects very little, and we'd rather you actually read it than skim past a wall of legal text.

What we collect

The only information this site collects is what you choose to type into the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service you're interested in
  • Your message

We don't use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or any hidden data collection. There are no user accounts, and nothing you browse on this site is logged or stored by us.

How the contact form actually works

This is the most important part of this policy. When you submit the contact form, it sends your message directly — no email app needs to open, and nothing depends on you having a mail client configured. The submission is delivered straight to swamy.kumar02@gmail.com through a form-handling service (Formspree), which relays it to that inbox without us running our own server or database to receive it.

Formspree processes the submission only to deliver it to us — it does not use your information for any other purpose. We don't see or store your message anywhere except in that one email. If you don't click "Send message," nothing is submitted at all.

Third-party services this site uses

This site loads a small number of resources from third parties, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts — used for the typography on this page. Google's servers may log standard technical information (like your IP address) when your browser requests these font files, per Google's own privacy policy.
  • Wikimedia Commons — used to load the Earth texture image on the homepage, under standard web-image request logging practices.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic server access data (like IP addresses) as part of standard web hosting, per GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and delivers them to our inbox. Formspree may log standard technical information as part of handling the submission, per Formspree's own privacy policy.

We don't control these third parties' data practices directly — we've linked their own policies above for full detail.

Your rights

Since we don't store any of your data on our own servers, there's nothing for us to delete, correct, or export on request — the only copy of your message exists in your own email account (in your Sent folder, and in ours as a received email) once you choose to send it.

If you'd like a message you previously sent us removed from our inbox, just ask — email us at swamy.kumar02@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business and professional use and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add analytics or a real backend form service in the future — we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and describe the change here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled: swamy.kumar02@gmail.com
